Our story

About Saga Keep Co.

Saga Keep Co. is an independent Florida-based TCG accessories brand founded by Karson Konkle. We create original playmat artwork inspired by mythology, legendary journeys, and public-domain literature.

Our first collection follows the fall of Troy and the voyage of Odysseus. The series begins with The Trojan Horse, continues with planned scenes such as The Lotus-Eaters, and later reaches The Nekyia.

What makes Saga Keep different

We are building each release as part of one connected journey. Artwork, packaging, product names, and future drops are designed to feel like pieces of the same saga.

Our current stage

Saga Keep Co. is a new brand preparing its first physical product release. We will publish real prototype photographs, final specifications, production timelines, and accurate availability before checkout opens.
