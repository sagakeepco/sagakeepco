Shipping and Preorders

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Pre-launch products

Products shown before checkout opens are still in development. Saga Keep Co. will publish final product specifications, real prototype photographs, pricing, and estimated fulfillment dates before accepting payment.

Preorders

A product will be clearly labeled “Preorder” when it is not ready for immediate shipment. The estimated production and shipping window will be displayed before checkout. Customers will receive an order confirmation after payment and a separate shipping confirmation with tracking after dispatch.

In-stock orders

Products labeled “In Stock” are intended to ship within the processing window shown on the product page. Business days exclude weekends and federal holidays.

Shipping area

Saga Keep Co. currently plans to ship within the United States. Available destinations and shipping charges will be shown during checkout.

Delays

Production and carrier delays can occur. When a material delay affects a preorder, Saga Keep Co. will provide an update using the email address associated with the order.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate delivery address. Contact support promptly when a correction is needed before shipment.
