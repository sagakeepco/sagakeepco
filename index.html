Release path

A collection told in story order.

Each release will represent a major moment from the fall of Troy and Odysseus's voyage home. The order below reflects the planned direction of the collection, not numbered chapters in the original poem.

First release

The Trojan Horse

The war ends through deception, and Troy falls in fire and shadow.

Next planned mat

The Lotus-Eaters

A storm drives the fleet to an island where the lotus strips Odysseus's men of every memory of home. He must drag them back to the ships before they surrender completely to forgetting.

Later release

The Nekyia

Much later in the voyage, Odysseus enters the realm of the dead to seek the prophet Tiresias and learn what still stands between him and Ithaca.

About the brand

Built for players who collect worlds.

Saga Keep Co. is an independent Florida-based TCG accessories brand founded by Karson Konkle. We create original artwork inspired by mythology, legendary journeys, and public-domain literature. Our goal is to make every playmat feel like part of one connected saga.
